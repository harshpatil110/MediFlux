## Step 1: Single-Unit Retail POS Sale -> Automated 4-Tier Cascade `[0:30 - 1:30]`

### Core Proposition
Demonstrate instantaneous demand propagation up the entire supply chain triggered by a single medicine dispense at a retail pharmacy counter.

### Action
```powershell
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/api/v1/simulator/pos `
  -H "Authorization: Bearer mock-admin-token" `
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" `
  -d '{"mode":"single","quantity":1,"product_id":"p-001-paracetamol","actor_role":"pharmacist"}'
```

### UI Navigation
- Navigate to **POS Simulator** screen (`/pos`).
- Select **Paracetamol 650mg** (1 strip).
- Click **Dispense 1 Strip**.
- Observe the **4-Tier Demand Propagation Cascade** illuminate in real time:
  1. **Pharmacy Edge**: Stock falls below reorder point → emits `ReplenishmentSignal(qty=25)`.
  2. **Stockist Hub**: Aggregates pharmacy demand → reserves regional safety stock → creates Purchase Order.
  3. **Distributor Layer**: Consolidates orders across stockists → dispatches delivery fleet route.
  4. **Manufacturer Plant**: Evaluates plant capacity → reschedules packaging line → commits production batch.

### Narrator Talking Points
> *"In legacy pharma supply chains, stockouts happen because sales data takes 3 to 7 days to reach manufacturers via batch invoices. In MediFlux, dispensing a single strip of Paracetamol triggers an autonomous LangGraph agent cascade that reserves stock at the stockist, dispatches logistics at the distributor, and reschedules production at the plant in under 50 milliseconds."*
